03 · Staff — Scheduling

Unified boarding + daycare calendar

Boarding stays render as multi-day spans, daycare as day chips, both against a per-day capacity meter. Pending online requests appear as dashed holds until approved — capacity math includes them so the desk never oversells a weekend. The event model is service-type-generic so grooming and training drop in at Phase 2 without rework.

05 · Client — Installable PWA

The client app: book, watch, pay

Three moments in the client loop. Home leads with the pet's current stay and one-tap rebooking against real availability; the report card lands as a push notification mid-stay with photos and the care log; checkout is a saved card, a tip, and done. This is the surface competitors gate behind a $300 add-on — here it ships in every paid tier.

06 · Platform — Per-tenant theming

White-label: one PWA, every brand

The same Home screen under two tenants. Each business's theme — logo, name, color tokens — is a config object the PWA loads at runtime, so upgrading to a paid tier flips branding without a deploy. Competitors charge $300 setup + $99/mo for this; here it's a row in the tenant's schema.
